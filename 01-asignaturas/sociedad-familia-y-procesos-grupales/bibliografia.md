# Bibliografía — Sociedad, familia y procesos grupales

Referencias principales utilizadas en los apuntes de Psicología Social y Sociología. Se incluyen obras clásicas y de referencia en dinámica de grupos, liderazgo, cognición social y relaciones intergrupales.

## Psicología Social — Dinámica de grupos y liderazgo

- Allport, G. W. (1954). *The nature of prejudice*. Addison-Wesley.
- Asch, S. E. (1946). Forming impressions of personality. *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology*, 41, 258–290.
- Asch, S. E. (1951). Effects of group pressure upon the modification and distortion of judgments. In H. Guetzkow (Ed.), *Groups, leadership and men*. Carnegie Press.
- Bales, R. F. (1950). *Interaction process analysis: A method for the study of small groups*. Addison-Wesley.
- Bass, B. M. (1985). *Leadership and performance beyond expectations*. Free Press.
- Brown, M. E., Treviño, L. K., & Harrison, D. A. (2005). Ethical leadership: A social learning perspective for construct development and testing. *Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes*, 97, 117–134.
- Cameron, K. S. (2013). *Practicing positive leadership: Tools and techniques that create extraordinary results*. Berrett-Koehler.
- Deutsch, M., & Gerard, H. B. (1955). A study of normative and informational social influences upon individual judgment. *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology*, 51, 629–636.
- French, J. R. P., & Raven, B. (1959). The bases of social power. In D. Cartwright (Ed.), *Studies in social power*. University of Michigan.
- Hall, E. T. (1966). *The hidden dimension*. Doubleday.
- Janis, I. L. (1972). *Victims of groupthink*. Houghton Mifflin.
- Johnson, D. W., & Johnson, R. T. (various). Learning together and alone / Cooperative learning.
- Kelley, H. H. (1973). The processes of causal attribution. *American Psychologist*, 28, 107–128.
- Kelman, H. C. (1958). Compliance, identification, and internalization: Three processes of attitude change. *Journal of Conflict Resolution*, 2, 51–60.
- Lewin, K. (various). Field theory and group dynamics.
- Lewin, K., Lippitt, R., & White, R. K. (1939). Patterns of aggressive behavior in experimentally created “social climates”. *Journal of Social Psychology*, 10, 271–299.
- Milgram, S. (1974). *Obedience to authority*. Harper & Row.
- Moreno, J. L. (various). Sociometry and group structure.
- Moreland, R. L., & Levine, J. M. (1982). Socialization in small groups: Temporal changes in individual-group relations. In L. Berkowitz (Ed.), *Advances in experimental social psychology* (Vol. 15). Academic Press.
- Moscovici, S. (various). Minority influence and social representations.
- Palacín, M., & Aiger, M. (2014). Modelo de desarrollo grupal: Confianza → Conflicto → Cohesión → Cooperación.
- Pettigrew, T. F., & Tropp, L. R. (2006). A meta-analytic test of intergroup contact theory. *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*, 90, 751–783.
- Pujolàs, P. (various). Aprendizaje cooperativo.
- Rosenthal, R., & Jacobson, L. (1968). *Pygmalion in the classroom*. Holt, Rinehart & Winston.
- Stogdill, R. M. (1948). Personal factors associated with leadership: A survey of the literature. *Journal of Psychology*, 25, 35–71.
- Tajfel, H., & Turner, J. C. (1979). An integrative theory of intergroup conflict. In W. G. Austin & S. Worchel (Eds.), *The social psychology of intergroup relations*. Brooks/Cole.
- Tuckman, B. W. (1965). Developmental sequence in small groups. *Psychological Bulletin*, 63, 384–399.
- Walumbwa, F. O., Avolio, B. J., Gardner, W. L., Wernsing, T. S., & Peterson, S. J. (2008). Authentic leadership: Development and validation of a theory-based measure. *Journal of Management*, 34, 89–126.
- Weiner, B. (1985). An attributional theory of achievement motivation and emotion. *Psychological Review*, 92, 548–573.

## Sociología y educación

- Coleman, J. S. (various). Capital social y educación.
- Durkheim, É. (various). Educación y socialización.
- Otras referencias específicas de familia, género e interculturalidad según los apuntes de Sociología.
